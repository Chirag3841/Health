Health Assessment Portal
Motto
"Empowering Health Awareness Through Personalized Insights"

This project aims to provide users with an easy-to-use tool for evaluating key health metrics, offering actionable recommendations to improve well-being and encouraging proactive health management.

Key Features
>Comprehensive Health Assessment

Analyzes blood pressure, sugar levels, and BMI to determine health status.

>Personalized Recommendations

Provides tailored health advice based on individual metrics.

>Medication & Supplement Guidance

Suggests appropriate medications with dosages and pricing (if applicable).

>Nutrition & Lifestyle Tips

Offers dietary recommendations and healthy habits for better well-being.

>Risk Level Visualization

Displays a color-coded risk assessment (Low/Medium/High) with a progress bar.

>Emergency Contact Info (For High Risk)

Shows emergency helplines if health risks are critical.

>Printable Reports

Users can print or save their health assessment for future reference.

>Responsive & User-Friendly

Works seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

Why This Project?
This tool bridges the gap between basic health awareness and actionable insights, helping users make informed decisions about their well-being.
